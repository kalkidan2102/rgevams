About ESSGI

The Ethiopian Space Science and Geospatial Institute leads Ethiopia’s national program in geospatial intelligence, geodesy, remote sensing, satellite systems, and geohazard monitoring.

Our mandate combines scientific observatories, emergency coordination, early-warning analysis, and civil-protection research to support safer infrastructure and stronger disaster awareness across the nation.

03 Field Observatories
115 Volcanic Centers
24/7 Monitoring Operations
01

Seismic Intelligence

The system collects, normalizes, and visualizes seismic activity across Ethiopia to identify fault corridors, tremor clusters, and public-risk conditions.

02

Volcanic Risk Monitoring

ESSGI tracks volcanic zones, crater behavior, gas emissions, thermal anomalies, and regional hazard posture for emergency planning.

03

Geospatial Decision Support

The portal models geospatial coverage, infrastructure impact zones, GNSS readings, and GIS-driven response intelligence.

04

Early Warning and Public Safety

Outputs from the system are used to build dashboards, technical advisories, operational alerts, and geohazard communication flows.
